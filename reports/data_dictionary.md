## 3. Data Integrity & Business Validation Rules

1. **Revenue Integrity Equation**:
   $$\text{Sales} = \text{ROUND}(\text{Quantity} \times \text{Unit\_Price} \times (1 - \text{Discount}), 2)$$
2. **Gross Margin Integrity Equation**:
   $$\text{Profit} = \text{ROUND}(\text{Sales} - \text{Cost}, 2)$$
3. **Profit Margin Percentage**:
   $$\text{Profit\_Margin} = \text{ROUND}\left(\frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{Sales}} \times 100, 2\right)$$
4. **Range Validations**:
   - $\text{Quantity} \ge 1$
   - $\text{Unit\_Price} > 0$
   - $0.00 \le \text{Discount} \le 0.30$
   - $\text{Sales} > 0$
   - $\text{Cost} > 0$
5. **Deduplication Rule**:
   - `Order_ID` is strictly unique in the processed dataset. All 25 duplicated test records from raw ingestion were audited and dropped.
6. **Null Handling**:
   - Missing discounts in raw files were imputed to `0.00` (no discount).
   - Whitespace in `Customer_Name`, `Product`, `Category`, and location fields were sanitized using bidirectional string trimming.
